# The One Nail Salon – Silver Spring, MD

A luxury, modern website built for **The One Nail Salon** located in Silver Spring, Maryland. Designed to capture the exact look, feel, and structure of [Inspire Nail Bar Rockville](https://inspirenailbarrockville.com/), customized with business information from Yelp and photography connected to the official Instagram page.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Design System & Aesthetic**:
   - **Luxury Gold & Ink Palette**: `#E2B33D` (Primary Gold), `#F7C42D` (Secondary Gold), `#000000` (Ink Black), and refined gradients.
   - **Typography**: Google Fonts pairing with *Poppins* for sleek modern body copy and buttons, *Allura* for cursive gold script flourishes, and *Archivo* for section headings.
   - **Full Responsiveness**: Mobile drawer menu, fluid typography, and touch-friendly interactive carousels.

2. **Core Components**:
   - **Sticky Top Header**: Gradient backdrop, custom high-resolution SVG brand emblem, animated navigation links, and a gold "Book Now" CTA pill button.
   - **Sticky Quick-Action Sidebar**: One-click direct calling `(301) 592-8888`, Google Maps directions, and Instagram profile access.
   - **3D Coverflow Hero Carousel**: Swiper-inspired 3D coverflow with infinite auto-rotation, perspective depth, and responsive scaling.
   - **Dark Info Bar**: Business hours, click-to-call phone number, and address linking directly to Google Maps navigation.
   - **Masonry Photo Gallery**: Custom column-balanced masonry with a lightbox image viewer supporting keyboard navigation (`Esc`, `←`, `→`).
   - **Instagram Showcase Feed**: Dedicated feature highlighting [@theonenailbar2024](https://www.instagram.com/theonenailbar2024/) with recent work and follow CTA.
   - **Testimonials Carousel**: Smooth auto-sliding review cards featuring feedback from Yelp Elite and local Silver Spring customers.
   - **Interactive Booking Modal**: Popup booking request form with date/time picker, technician preferences (e.g., Lena), service selection, and instant confirmation.

3. **Pages Included**:
   - `index.html`: Home page with full hero, info bar, about story, service highlights, gallery, Instagram showcase, testimonials, and 4-column footer.
   - `services.html`: Itemized service menu with prices, descriptions, and dotted price separators across 6 categories (Pedicure Bar, Manicure Bar, Enhancements, Add-ons, Kid's Spa, Waxing).
   - `gallery.html`: Filterable photo gallery by category (All, Manicures, Spa Pedicures, Nail Art & Gel-X, Instagram Highlights) with lightbox.
   - `contact.html`: Contact info cards, inquiry submission form, embedded Google map, and links to Yelp & Instagram.

## 🚀 How to View Locally

You can open `index.html` directly in any web browser, or launch a lightweight HTTP server:

```bash
# Python 3
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000) in your browser.
