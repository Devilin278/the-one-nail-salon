The One Nail Salon

Logo Design Concepts

Three bespoke logo interpretations featuring an encompassing open heart in a romantic red-pinkish gradient, seamlessly embracing the brand typography.

Preview Background:

Concept 1: Calligraphic Ribbon Embrace

Continuous Flourish

Aesthetic: A fluid, continuous calligraphic ribbon heart that arcs gently over the shoulders, flows down the sides, sweeps under the location text, and curls inward at the top right with an open romantic flourish. Cradles the entire typography in a radiant rose-crimson gradient.

Concept 2: Modern Sculpted Dual-Arc

High-Fashion Contemporary

Aesthetic: Inspired by luxury haute-couture fashion houses. Features two sculpted, overlapping curved arcs that frame the top-left and bottom-right edges of the logo with an open gap and an accent jewel at the top dip. Clean, sleek, and strikingly modern.

Concept 3: Signature Menu-Inspired Heart

Artistic & Hand-Drawn

Aesthetic: Directly inspired by the gestural hand-drawn open heart drawn on the physical salon menu booklet cover. Features warm, organic freeform curves with an open sketch crossover at the top and an expressive tail taper at the bottom, rendered in velvet rose-pink.
