About Us

Feel good about being beautiful!

At The One Nail Salon in Silver Spring, MD, you can find a highly motivated and talented team of nail technicians and therapists dedicated to providing you with a welcoming atmosphere of pure relaxation and a first-class pampering experience not only the first time, but every single visit.

We combine immaculate sanitation, artistry, and modern nail care with the best organic products. Whether you desire a therapeutic spa pedicure with herbal foot soaks and hot stone massage, a long-lasting dip powder manicure, Apres Gel-X extensions, or custom creative nail art, The One Nail Salon is your ultimate sanctuary for beauty and rejuvenation.
